The Final Case Study

Statute-Barred Debt Governance in Secondary Debt Markets & Operational Debt Servicing.

The Statute-Barred Dilemma

Under the Limitation Act 1980 (and equivalent global consumer credit laws), unsecured debts generally become statute-barred after 6 years of continuous debtor inactivity (no payment and no written acknowledgment). Once a debt crosses this legal threshold:

  • Complete Loss of Legal Recourse: Creditors and collection agencies are permanently prohibited from initiating court proceedings or pursuing enforcement orders.
  • Severe Regulatory Penalties: Threatening legal action on a statute-barred debt constitutes an explicit regulatory violation (FCA CONC 7.15, CFPB Fair Debt Collection Practices Act), triggering fines and licensing revocation.
  • Phantom Portfolio Overvaluation: Secondary debt buyers frequently acquire debt pools without realizing that 70% or more of the accounts are legally dead, leading to catastrophic capital misallocation.
Team Gryffindor's AI Strategy

Rather than relying on manual audit samples or delayed legal review, our deep neural architecture introduces instantaneous portfolio triage at the point of ingestion:

  • Informative Missingness Exploitation: 97.9% of accounts lack formal insolvency filings, and 74.4% lack recent payment method records. Rather than dropping these fields, encoding explicit missingness turned sparse data into high-value legal signals.
  • Pre-Purchase Valuation Gate: Run loan tapes through Model 3 prior to bid submission to haircut purchase price by the exact fraction of unenforceable principal.
  • Intelligent Agent Routing: Automatically route Class 0 (Active) accounts to priority recovery queues, and funnel Class 1 (Barred) accounts to compliant, soft-contact or write-off workflows.
